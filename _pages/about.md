I am Xiao Xue, also known as Sean. My work sits across human-computer interaction, design research, participatory design, and emerging technology.

My research explores how people understand, negotiate, and reshape technology in everyday contexts, including smart home ecosystems, human-vehicle-home integration, deepfake scams, and AI-supported design practices.

## Research Interests

- Human-computer interaction
- Participatory design and co-design
- Smart home and connected ecosystems
- Human-centered AI and LLMs
- Design probes and research through design
