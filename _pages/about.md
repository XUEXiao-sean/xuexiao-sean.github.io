> Design research is about nomadism, reflection and "do tanks".

I am an emerging design researcher working at the intersection of **Design** and **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)**, exploring how technological systems can be thoughtfully designed to empower people, especially through participatory approaches that invite "outsiders" to engage in design conversations.

I am currently a **long-term research intern at The Future Laboratory, Tsinghua University**, where I contribute to both industry-facing and speculative projects that situate emerging technologies such as **smart homes, autonomous vehicles, and large language models** within everyday social contexts.

Grounded in **Research through Design (RtD)**, my research draws on designerly methods to generate knowledge about sociotechnical conditions. I experiment with techniques such as probes, co-design workshops, and enactments to engage non-expert publics in collaboratively imagining, shaping, and critiquing technologies. These projects surface real-world problems of agency, technical ethics, and power dynamics related to emerging technology, while reflecting on how design practices can challenge solutionist narratives in technology-dominated fields and HCI.

I hold a multidisciplinary background in **multimedia technologies** and **design informatics**, with training in machine learning, game development, and qualitative research. I am now actively **seeking PhD opportunities** where I can further develop these interests and collaborate on new questions. Please feel free to contact me if you are interested in my research.

## Research Interests

- Participatory design, co-design, and research through design
- Smart homes, autonomous vehicles, and connected everyday ecosystems
- Human-centered AI, large language models, and design-in-use
- Technology ethics, agency, and power in sociotechnical systems
- Speculative, critical, and public-facing design methods
